      Intelligence notes

      The DSM’s most important artifact: what the customer says, what it indicates, and what leadership needs to know. Internal only, written as facts and behavior.

      Escalation memo

      Escalate with a recommendation: facts, impact, options, recommendation and the decision needed. Drafted from the most severe open blocker.

      DSM playbook

      The operating culture of the Deployment Strategy Team, with a live self-check on this account.

      Call scribe agent

      Turns a customer call into draft decisions, actions and an intelligence note, whether Phenom recorded it, the customer shared their own transcript, or nothing was recorded and the DSM debriefs it by voice or typing.

      Guardrails: drafts only, nothing enters the record until the DSM accepts it. Notes describe facts and behavior, never emotions or personal characterizations. Stakeholders are referred to by role.
